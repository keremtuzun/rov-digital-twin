# Inspection terms

- `normal_surface`: no model-visible concern in the current frame; this is not proof of integrity.
- `biofouling`: visible biological accumulation that may justify closer inspection.
- `marine_debris`: debris or litter in the scene; it is an operational anomaly, not structural damage.
- `low_visibility`: image evidence is degraded by lighting, turbidity, blur, or occlusion.
- `possible_damage`: a visual pattern of concern that requires human review.
- `possible_weak_point`: a localized concern; never describe it as a confirmed failure.
- `unknown`: insufficient or out-of-distribution evidence.
