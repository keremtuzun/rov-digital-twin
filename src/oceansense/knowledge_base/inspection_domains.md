# Inspection domains

- `structure`: piers, docks, hulls, pipes, tanks, aquaculture infrastructure, and coastal assets.
- `nature_ecology`: coral, seafloor, marine vegetation, habitat, and visible biodiversity indicators.
- `contamination`: debris, turbidity, discoloration, sheen, or algae-bloom-like visual indicators.
- `fishing_aquaculture`: fish activity, habitat clues, nets, cages, and survey conditions.
- `general_underwater`: underwater imagery without a validated specialized domain.
- `unknown`: insufficient evidence for a domain classification.
